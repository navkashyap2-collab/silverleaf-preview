Retail, commercial and hospitality property, from Cantonment Street

Silverleaf Investments Pty Ltd invests in, develops and manages retail, commercial and hospitality property. It is based on Cantonment Street in Fremantle’s West End, with further holdings in Perth and Hobart.

Founder Gerard O’Brien had been developing and redeveloping property around central Fremantle for about 25 years by 2020. The work is mostly in the West End: heritage buildings on High Street, Market Street and Cantonment Street, the Woolstores centre, and the former police headquarters, which reopened as Garde Hotel in 2025.

Outside Fremantle it bought Bull Creek Central from Stockland in 2022 and manages Armadale Central, and has been connected with Hobart’s Cat and Fiddle Arcade since 2010.

Across those projects the work covers buying property, restoring heritage buildings for new uses, and managing and leasing centres, with outside architects on each project. On its leasing profile, Silverleaf describes itself as 100% Australian-owned and operated, with projects across the commercial, industrial and retail sectors. Its leasing team has also let office and medical suites in Hobart, at 22 Elizabeth Street and the Fitzgerald Building, 85 Collins Street.

Fremantle

West End

Why Fremantle

Much of the West End was built in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries, and several of these buildings had empty upper floors

Silverleaf’s projects have tended to keep that fabric and put it back to work: offices above shops, a new arcade through an old block, a hotel inside police quarters built from 1897.
