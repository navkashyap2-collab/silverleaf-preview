The story of Manning Buildings

Upper floors back in use

The Manning Buildings were built between 1902 and 1906 for the Manning Bros Estate. Parts of the first floor had been empty for decades. The redevelopment brought the upper floors of six buildings back as commercial offices and refreshed the street-level shops.

Photo: Sam Wilson, Wikimedia Commons

Restoring the facades

Paint was stripped from the upper facades back to the original lime render, and windows were remade to match the originals. Surviving shopfronts were kept and new ones designed to sit with them.

A new arcade and landscaped space now link High Street to Paddy Troy Lane, improving the walk between the Fremantle Markets and the West End.

Photo: Meyer Shircore Architects (project page)
