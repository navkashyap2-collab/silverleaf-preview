Review notes

Private website concept prepared for Silverleaf Investments’ review. Not an official company website.

This concept was prepared independently and offered without charge. Silverleaf has not commissioned, reviewed or approved it. Nothing has been published, and the existing silverleaf.net.au domain, DNS and email are untouched.

Facts are drawn from the public sources below, checked on 8 October 2026. Where sources describe ownership, development or management, the site keeps those relationships separate.

The pages follow the layout, type scale and motion of era-residence.com, rebuilt for this concept with Silverleaf’s own content; no Era copy, images, video, fonts or code are reused. The fonts, cut-outs, map data and libraries that stand in for Era’s are listed below.

A note for Silverleaf

What this is

A private preview of how a new Silverleaf website could look and read: 9 properties, each with its own page, an about page, leasing contacts and a contact page. It was built as a free, unsolicited concept. You are under no obligation, and nothing goes live unless you ask for it.

What is real, what is not

Every fact comes from a public source listed below. The wording is proposed. 18 photographs are openly licensed; 12 are third-party reference images (marked on the page) that need your permission or replacing. The Woolstores render is labelled as an artist’s impression. The enquiry form sends nothing.

What you would decide

Whether you like the direction; the wording and which properties appear; staff names and contact details; photographs (yours, or licences for the reference images); and, only if you want to go ahead, where the site is hosted and where enquiries go. The checklist below lists each item.

To approve before any launch

  • All company copy. Every statement written in Silverleaf’s voice is proposed wording.
  • Logo. The two-leaf mark and “Silverleaf” wordmark are a vector redraw of Silverleaf’s logo, traced from the 105 × 65 px version on woolstores.com.au, with colours sampled from a supplied copy (leaf green #A2CC3A, grey #A7A9AC) and the wordmark set in Tinos, an open-licence Times-style serif. Replace with Silverleaf’s master artwork and confirm the brand colours.
  • Robert O’Brien’s formal title. Public profiles confirm his leasing and development role but not a board title.
  • Publishing staff names, roles, mobile numbers and email addresses (taken from the Woolstores leasing page and realestate.com.au).
  • Team bios. Gerard O’Brien’s is drawn from Business News and Development Ready. Kyle Hammond’s career dates come from his LinkedIn profile and data-aggregator listings (low confidence), so he should confirm them. Greg Radin and Craig Olde have no bios because no public source describes their background.
  • The general office number. 08 9335 5492 is published as the centre management line for Woolstores, Bull Creek Central and Armadale Central.
  • How each property relationship is described, in particular current ownership of Atwell Arcade and the Manning Buildings, which this concept does not assert.
  • Whether 26 South Terrace, Bull Creek Central and Armadale Central should appear, and whether other properties should be added.
  • Licences for every image marked “permission required”, or replacement photography supplied by Silverleaf. The Bull Creek Central and Armadale Central photos come from the centres’ own websites, which Silverleaf manages, so Silverleaf can likely approve them directly.
  • Attribution wording for the Creative Commons images, or replacement with owned photography.
  • Enquiry routing, a privacy policy and a form back end. The form is a demo and sends nothing.
  • Domain, hosting and removal of the noindex settings. Not included in the complimentary offer.

Existing web presence

  • silverleaf.net.au resolves and carries the company’s Microsoft 365 email. Its web address answers plain HTTP with a forwarding-service 404 (“does not have a redirection page”) and fails the HTTPS handshake, so no corporate website is published there as of 8 October 2026.
  • The realcommercial.com.au agency profile carries an “Agency website” link that points to an empty address (http://www./).

Deliberately left out

Woolstores residential proposal ($65m)
Reported withdrawn in September 2024 (Business News). Not presented as a current development.
Woolstores “Completion 2024” and future-tense development copy
Pre-construction wording still on woolstores.com.au. The centre has traded since April 2025, so the site describes it in the present tense.
Woolstores hotel component
Earlier approvals included hotel rooms. No source confirms a hotel in the completed building, so none is mentioned.
“Fremantle’s first Green Star-rated office building” (Atwell)
Single blog source; no Green Building Council record checked. Sustainability credentials are not claimed.
Warders’ Cottages and other properties named in earlier press
No current source confirms Silverleaf ownership. The state heritage register lists the State Housing Commission for the Warders’ Cottages.
Sales yield for 26 South Terrace (5.89%)
Public, but left to Silverleaf to decide whether to publish. The $7.65 million price is shown, as supplied in the company profile.
Large headline figures in the style of luxury agency sites (sales totals, portfolio value)
Silverleaf publishes no such figures. The home page figures are only the verified ones: properties featured, cities, rooms, tenancies, floor area and years developing in Fremantle.
